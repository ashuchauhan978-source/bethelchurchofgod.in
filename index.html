About Us

A Place to Worship, Pray & Grow

Come as you are and join us as we seek God together as a church family.

Our Church Family

Bethel Church of God Ministries is a Christian church in Heera Nagar, Ludhiana, Punjab. Our desire is to provide a welcoming place for worship, prayer, fellowship, Bible learning and service.

“Let us come together in faith, encourage one another, and grow in God's Word.”

Everyone Is Welcome

Whether you are visiting a church for the first time or looking for a church family, we invite you to worship with us. Our regular Sunday service is from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
